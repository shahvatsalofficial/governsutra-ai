## Repository AI discovery and inventory bootstrap

Inspect allowlisted manifests and AI/tool declarations, create candidate records, show confidence and ask owners to confirm before promoting to authoritative inventory.

## AI system registration and ownership

Collect intended use, owner, risk owner, model/tool versions, data sources, deployment context, affected people and human checkpoints; validate required fields.

## Contextual risk and impact review

Calculate transparent risk indicators, assess impacts, record unknowns, assign controls and route risk acceptance to an authorized owner.

## Model, prompt, tool or plugin change review

Diff versions and permissions, identify affected systems, request new tests/evidence and require owner review before activation.

## Data source and data-flow review

Record source categories, origins, access, retention and transformations; flag sensitive categories for specialist/legal review without inferring lawful basis.

## Human checkpoint and escalation validation

Test that checkpoints have named roles, time bounds, override ability, escalation and auditable intervention evidence.

## Control evidence refresh and expiry

Find stale or missing evidence, request renewal, verify hashes and provenance, record reviewer attestation and expiry.

## Release, periodic review and retirement

Aggregate changes and unresolved risks, produce a scoped review packet, record human decision, and preserve suspension/retirement evidence.
