# Framework Mapping Strategy — Not a Compliance Claim

GovernSutra can help users organize evidence against selected frameworks, but it must not decide legal applicability or declare compliance. Mapping packs must be versioned, source-linked, reviewed by qualified experts and scoped to the organization's context.

Potential reference structures:
- **NIST AI RMF 1.0**: Govern, Map, Measure, Manage. NIST notes the framework functions organize risk-management outcomes and are not a simple checklist. Official resources: https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework and https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/ai/NIST.AI.100-1.pdf
- **ISO/IEC 42001:2023**: AI management system requirements and continual improvement. Official standard page: https://www.iso.org/standard/42001. The full standard is copyrighted; do not reproduce its text without rights.
- **EU AI Act**: progressive application dates and obligations depend on system role, intended purpose, jurisdiction, amendments and facts. Official Commission overview: https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/regulatory-framework-ai. Obtain qualified legal advice for applicability.
- **MCP security**: tool access and data sharing require explicit consent, least privilege and secure authorization. Official specification: https://modelcontextprotocol.io/specification/2026-07-28

A future mapping pack may store framework ID, edition/version, source URL, mapping rationale, reviewer, legal review date, jurisdiction and evidence links. The UI must label mappings as informational until approved by the user's qualified reviewers.
