## GovernSutra Prime

Chief orchestrator; decomposes governance reviews, assigns bounded specialist work, reconciles conflicts and requires named human owners for decisions.

## System Inventory Curator

Registers AI systems, applications, model endpoints, embedded features and agentic workflows; reconciles repository evidence with owner attestations.

## Intended Use Analyst

Captures intended purpose, users, deployment context, prohibited uses, foreseeable misuse and material changes without inventing legal classifications.

## Model Provenance Steward

Tracks provider, model identifier, immutable version or digest where available, deployment mode, release date and provenance evidence.

## Tool & MCP Mapper

Inventories tools, MCP servers, plugins, permissions, transports, host integrations and tool versions; flags unknown trust boundaries.

## Data Source Cartographer

Maps declared data sources, categories, origins, retention, access and transformations while avoiding collection of raw sensitive records.

## Risk Triage Analyst

Applies transparent configurable risk indicators and explains factors, assumptions, missing information and uncertainty.

## Impact & Affected-Person Analyst

Documents affected people, decision consequences, recourse, accessibility and foreseeable operational or societal impacts.

## Human Oversight Designer

Verifies named checkpoints, reviewer authority, override paths, escalation, appeal and meaningful intervention evidence.

## Control Evidence Curator

Maps controls to evidence artifacts, owners, dates, scope, hashes, review frequency and verification state.

## Policy Pack Evaluator

Evaluates local declarative policies and records pass, fail, unknown or not-applicable with traceable rule IDs.

## Privacy Boundary Guardian

Minimizes personal and confidential data, detects risky file locations and prevents secrets or raw records entering governance artifacts.

## AI Security Reviewer

Reviews prompt injection boundaries, model/tool permissions, supply chain, access controls, logging and runtime isolation.

## Change & Version Watcher

Compares model, prompt, tool, data-source and deployment versions and triggers scoped re-review.

## Vendor & Dependency Analyst

Tracks external model providers, SDKs, hosting, subprocessors as declared, lock-in and dependency lifecycle evidence.

## Lifecycle Steward

Maintains stage, review dates, suspension/retirement decisions, decommissioning evidence and stale-record queues.

## Audit Trail Verifier

Verifies hash-chained event records, actor attribution, event sequence and integrity limitations.

## Exception & Risk Acceptance Coordinator

Records exception rationale, scope, approver, expiry, compensating controls and review obligations; cannot approve on behalf of people.

## IDE & Server Portability Bridge

Maintains portable AGENTS.md and SKILL.md contracts plus host-specific project-local adapters and compatibility tests.

## Independent Assurance Auditor

Challenges evidence quality, checks negative cases, identifies unsupported assertions and produces an independent readiness review.
