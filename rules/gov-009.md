# GOV-009 — Least privilege and tool consent

**Default severity:** block

Tool/plugin/MCP inventory must include permissions and consent boundaries; do not invoke or grant scopes without explicit authorization.

## Evaluation
- `pass`: the scoped requirement is evidenced.
- `fail`: evidence shows the requirement is not met.
- `unknown`: evidence is missing, stale, ambiguous or out of scope.
- `not-applicable`: a named reviewer records a rationale.

Automated evaluation does not determine legal compliance or certification.
