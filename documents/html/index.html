How it works

From declarations to a human decision

You declare what a system is. The tool discovers what the repository shows, evaluates ten rules, and writes a report a reviewer can read. A person decides.

Register

Describe a system in JSON or commit a manifest.

governsutra init
governsutra register --input system.json
governsutra scan --repo .

Assess

Ten rules, each with a status and a reason.

governsutra assess --id MY-SYS --format html
governsutra evidence verify

Prove

Replay hostile-input checks; verify the seal.

governsutra audit --out ./audit
governsutra verify --report \
  ./audit/audit-report.json
Why it is different

Honest by construction

Compliance dashboards love green badges. GovernSutra shows what is unknown, stale or unverified next to every indicator.

Unknown is not pass

Missing, stale or unverifiable evidence is reported as unknown, never promoted to a pass.

Read more

Humans decide

Exceptions need a named human approver and an expiry. An agent approver is rejected.

Tamper-evident

Hash-chained audit log and sealed reports. Edit one entry and verification fails.

Know your limits

What the tool prepares, and what only a person decides

This line is the product. Everything on the left is deterministic and inspectable; everything on the right stays with accountable people.

The tool

May prepare

Everything it does is deterministic, offline and inspectable.

  • DOESRegister and validate declarations
  • DOESScan allowlisted files for candidates
  • DOESEvaluate ten rules with reasons
  • DOESHash evidence and chain audit events
  • DOESWrite MD, HTML and JSON reports
A named human

Must decide

No agent, script or score can take these over.

  • HUMANApprove an exception
  • HUMANAccept a risk or approve a release
  • HUMANDecide whether a law applies
  • HUMANConfirm a scan candidate is real
  • HUMANJudge whether evidence is true
Transparent scoring

A risk indicator you can interrogate

Six weighted factors, published missing-data behavior, and a sensitivity view that shows which assumption moves the score. It is a prioritization aid, never a legal category.

What it does not do

  • It is not legal advice, a regulatory classification, a certification or proof of safety.
  • The audit is automated self-verification, not a third-party audit. Hashes and seals detect edits; they are not signatures.
  • The 20 agents and 26 skills are instruction files. Native execution inside each IDE is not verified.
  • No dashboard, database, SSO or MCP server yet. Those are on the roadmap and are not shown as features.
