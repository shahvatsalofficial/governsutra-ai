Legal and Governance

This page is information about the project, not legal advice.

License#

Apache License 2.0 (LICENSE). Copyright 2026 Vatsal Shah. Patent grant and trademark limits per the license. No warranty.

Dependencies and third parties#

Zero runtime and zero development dependencies, so no transitive license obligations. Names of standards, IDEs, vendors and protocols are used only to describe compatibility or informational mapping; no affiliation or endorsement is implied. No standard text is reproduced (ISO/IEC 42001 is copyrighted). Fixtures are synthetic.

What the tool will not state#

It does not declare compliance, certification, regulatory classification or safety. Specifically for the EU AI Act, NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001 and OECD AI Principles: mapping is informational organization of evidence. Whether a law applies depends on legal facts, roles and intended purpose and requires qualified legal advice.

Privacy and data protection#

Local-only processing; no telemetry or network calls; metadata and hashes only. The tool does not process personal data by design. If your records name real people (owners), treat that as personal data under your own policy. Keep customer data, secrets and private prompts out of records and evidence.

Claims policy#

Every public statement in this repository must be backed by code, a test, or an explicit "not verified" label. Roadmap items are never shown as features. Marketing phrases such as "compliant", "certified", "guaranteed safe" are prohibited; an audit check scans generated reports for them.

Project governance#

Maintainer: Vatsal Shah. Changes by pull request with tests; the maintainer decides releases. See GOVERNANCE.md, CONTRIBUTING.md and CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md. Only humans approve exceptions and releases; no agent in this project may.

Responsible use#

Use the output to prepare evidence for qualified reviewers, not to replace them. High-impact domains (employment, credit, health, education, essential services, law enforcement) need domain, legal and ethics review regardless of any score.

Contact#

Questions: GitHub issues. Security: private advisory.
